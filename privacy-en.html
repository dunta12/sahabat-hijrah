Privacy Policy — Sahabat Hijrah

Last updated: September 2026

Sahabat Hijrah ("this app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we do — and do not — collect.

1. No accounts, no personal data

This app does not require registration and does not collect names, emails, or any personal information.

2. All data stays on your device

Prayer times, notes, tasbih counts, and settings are calculated and stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

3. Location

Location is used only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times. It is never uploaded or shared.

4. Internet usage

The app loads its content from a public web page (GitHub Pages). No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

5. Notifications

Prayer-time notifications are generated locally on your device and can be turned off at any time.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact us via the project page: https://github.com/dunta12
